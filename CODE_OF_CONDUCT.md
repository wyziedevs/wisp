# Code of conduct

Wisp is a project for the community. Be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work.

- Welcome newcomers and answer questions kindly.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. No harassment, personal attacks, slurs or discrimination.
- Do not publish other people's private information.
- Keep issues, pull requests and chat on topic.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, message a maintainer on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/2mxraHBVtB).
